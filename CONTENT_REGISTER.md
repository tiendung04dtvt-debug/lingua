# Lingua content and source register

**Scope:** the 29 November 2026 English exit-standard assessment for University of Danang students, administered through the University of Science and Education (UED) registration route. Lingua groups practice material by B1, B2, and C1 to support a C1 target.

**Access date:** 8 November 2026. This is a checked starter inventory, not a claim that every publicly available paper has been found. No public archive of the local assessment's live papers was found in this review. Treat recollected questions and unofficial “real exam” claims as unverified.

## Content policy for this project

- Keep provider, original exam, source URL, access date, skill, task, assigned level, level rationale, review state, and reuse notes attached to every imported item.
- Keep a source's original task order. Do not shuffle individual questions inside a passage or audio set. Practice-mode option shuffling is optional and must preserve fixed-position tasks.
- Lingua's public repository contains original demo items, app code, this source index, and links. It does not mirror complete copyrighted papers, audio, or answer keys.
- Personal materials may be imported from a local JSON pack into that browser's local storage. Imported material stays on the device and is not uploaded or synced.
- Use consistent `content_origin_type` labels: `official_local`, `official_related_format`, `official_other_exam`, `original_practice`, `original_demo`, `personal_material`, or `unverified`. Never mark an item verified before checking its source and answer key.
- Extra original practice variations such as reference, sentence insertion, and vocabulary-in-context are included to build transferable skills. Their presence does not claim that every UED paper uses a fixed count of each sub-type.
