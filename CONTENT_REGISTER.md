# Lingua content and source register

**Scope:** the 29 November 2026 English exit-standard assessment for University of Danang students, administered through the University of Science and Education (UED) registration route. Lingua groups practice material by B1, B2, and C1 to support a C1 target.

**Access date:** 8 November 2026. This is a checked starter inventory, not a claim that every publicly available paper has been found. No public archive of the local assessment's live papers was found in this review. Treat recollected questions and unofficial “real exam” claims as unverified.

## Content policy for this project

- Keep provider, original exam, source URL, access date, skill, task, assigned level, level rationale, review state, and reuse notes attached to every imported item.
- Keep a source's original task order. Do not shuffle individual questions inside a passage or audio set. Practice-mode option shuffling is optional and must preserve fixed-position tasks.
- Lingua's public repository contains original demo items, app code, this source index, and links. It does not mirror complete copyrighted papers, audio, or answer keys.
- Personal materials may be imported from a local JSON pack into that browser's local storage. Imported material stays on the device and is not uploaded or synced.
- Use consistent `content_origin_type` labels: `official_local`, `official_related_format`, `official_other_exam`, `original_practice`, `original_demo`, `personal_material`, or `unverified`. Never mark an item verified before checking its source and answer key.
- Extra original practice variations such as reference, sentence insertion, and vocabulary-in-context are included to build transferable skills. Their presence does not claim that every UED paper uses a fixed count of each sub-type.

The PDF presents a single Bậc 3–5 test, not separate B1, B2, and C1 papers. Its sample pages show Listening (8 short items, 3 conversations with 4 questions each, 3 talks/lectures with 5 questions each), Reading (4 passages with 10 questions each), Writing (email/letter and essay), and Speaking (social interaction, solution discussion, topic development). The answer key is in the closing pages. The official format assigns the result band from the rounded four-skill average; it does not label each sample item as B1, B2, or C1. Therefore the local 6-question-per-band diagnostic is original Lingua practice and its per-item level assignments are instructional estimates, not official classifications.

The UFL page hosts the PDF and all three audio files: [official UFL sample paper and audio](https://vstep.ufl.udn.vn/NLTA/Dinhdang/2025/De-thi-mau-Vstep-bac-3-52025.9.10067). Copies remain under the ignored `packs/private/` folder and are not included in public Git commits. The 2024 UFL specification page describes the official four-skill format and score mapping: [UFL Bậc 3–5 format](https://vstep.ufl.udn.vn/CDR/Dinhdang/2024/Dinh-dang-de-thi-danh-gia-nang-luc-su-dung-tieng-Anh-tu-bac-3-den-bac-5-theo-Khung-nang-luc-ngoai-ngu-6-bac-dung-cho-Viet-Nam--2024.12.33910).

These templates have overlapping task forms but are institution/course-exit documents, not UFL live papers or confirmed UED exam papers; keep them categorized as supplementary institutional references. The official UFL 2024 format and the other provider's word-count descriptions are not identical, so this app follows UFL's published specification for the target format and defers to UED's latest candidate instructions for local rules.

These are genuine published sample/practice papers from identifiable providers, not verified copies of the live monthly exams. No public UED-specific past-paper bank has been confirmed. Do not label a provider's sample as a UED past paper or infer official B1/B2/C1 tags for individual questions.

The Resources page now also links the learner's requested practice leads: ZIM, PREP and its linked Drive collections, VSTEP.edu.vn, Luyenthivstep.vn, Studocu, and two Facebook group posts. They are separated from the university-hosted sample library and labeled by what was actually checked. In particular, the PREP Drive folder title mentions 20 tests, but the files have not been enumerated/reviewed, and the Studocu/Facebook items remain user-shared or access-restricted sources. These are leads for practice, not 20 verified papers or confirmed UED live tests.

The learner-provided Drive archive is stored only under the ignored `packs/private/reference-materials/`: one compilation PDF titled “7 Vstep Tests (B1-B2-C1) – Full Key”, seven MP3 files, and three separate MP3 files. The author, provenance, test/audio mapping, and reproduction permission remain unverified. It is excluded from the public repository and public question bank.
